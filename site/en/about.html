About Aether EdTech

Two teachers, one codebase, one cause.

Aether EdTech began with a plain observation: a lot of good teaching never travels beyond the room it happens in. We want to change that, with software that carries it further.

Mission & vision

What we are here to do, and where we are heading.

Our mission

To support education and educational projects by building free learning tools, sharing technical and teaching skills with other initiatives, and funding this work through honest software and cybersecurity services.

Our vision

A world where good learning reaches everyone, whatever their starting point, and where the technology behind it is open, safe and trustworthy.

Our cause

Education that does not depend on where you were born.

Aether EdTech exists to support education and educational projects. That is a broad sentence, so here is what it means in practice.

We create learning resources that are free for the learner. We build and maintain the software behind them. And we take part in other initiatives, offering technical and teaching skills to people who are already doing good work and could use another pair of hands.

What we believe

  • Learning should be enjoyable. A student who is curious learns more than one who is afraid.
  • Access should not be a privilege. Core educational tools stay free to use.
  • Be honest about what we are. We are small, we are new, and we say so.
  • Reinvest, never extract. Any surplus goes back into educational work.
