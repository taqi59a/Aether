Non-profit · Education · Technology · Brussels

Good learning should reach everyone, wherever they start.

Aether EdTech is a Belgian non-profit (ASBL) for education and educational technology. We build free learning projects, protect organisations' digital assets with our cybersecurity expertise, and put every surplus back into education.

What we do

Three ways we work for education.

We are teachers and engineers. That mix shapes everything we make.

  1. We build learning projects

    Websites and software designed with the classroom in mind: clear, playful and free for the learner. BacMath.eu was the first. More projects are on the way.

  2. We build and protect for organisations

    Software development and cybersecurity for schools, associations and companies. Fees cover our costs, and any surplus goes back into free educational projects, because an ASBL cannot distribute profit.

  3. We join other initiatives

    Education is rarely fixed by one organisation alone. We contribute skills, software and time to projects run by schools, associations and community groups.

Cybersecurity

Protect what your organisation depends on.

Schools, associations and small companies hold more valuable data than they think, and are targeted more often than they expect. We help you know what you have, secure it, and be ready if something goes wrong.

01

Asset mapping

A clear inventory of your devices, accounts, data and software, and of who can reach what.

02

Security assessments

A practical review of your websites, systems and configuration, ending in a short, prioritised list of fixes.

03

Secure development

Software built with security from the first line, not added at the end.

04

Awareness and readiness

Training for staff and teachers, and a simple plan for the day an incident happens.

Our first project

BacMath.eu

A maths learning website that treats practice as play instead of punishment. Students learn at their own pace, in a format that makes them want to come back tomorrow.

It is free to use, and it is the first of several projects we are preparing. Every project we launch will be listed on our projects page.

  • Maths explained in a fun, approachable way
  • Open to every learner, no fee to start
  • Built and maintained by teachers and engineers
