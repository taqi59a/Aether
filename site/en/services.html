Services

Software development and cybersecurity services

We build software and strengthen the security of organisations' systems, from schools and associations to companies. Our background in education means we understand how schools and non-profits operate. Service income funds our free educational projects.

What we offer

Development, security and advice

  1. Websites and web applications

    Accessible, fast and maintainable websites and web tools for schools, associations and small organisations.

  2. Educational software

    Learning platforms, quizzes, practice tools and content systems, designed with teaching input from the start.

  3. Cybersecurity

    Asset mapping, security assessments, secure development, awareness training and incident readiness. Full details below.

  4. Digital advice

    Guidance on choosing tools, moving services online and making them easier and safer to use.

Cybersecurity services

Practical security for organisations without a dedicated security team

Each engagement is scoped to your organisation's size, systems and budget. We report findings in clear language, with fixes ranked by priority.

01

Asset inventory and classification

We list your devices, accounts, data, software and services, and rank them by importance to your organisation.

02

Vulnerability and configuration review

We check websites, applications, cloud settings and networks for weaknesses and explain each finding clearly.

03

Secure development and code review

We build software with security requirements from the start and review existing code before it reaches your users.

04

Account and access protection

Multi-factor authentication, appropriate permissions, password policy and backups that are tested to restore.

05

Awareness training

Short, practical sessions for staff and teachers on phishing, fraud and safe working habits.

06

Incident readiness

A written plan that sets out who does what during an incident, to limit disruption and data loss.

Confidentiality and authorisation. Every engagement is covered by a written agreement. We test only systems for which we hold written authorisation, and we treat all client information as confidential.
