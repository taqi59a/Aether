Before you give

Information for donors

Key facts about our status and how donations are used.

  • Registered ASBL. Aether EdTech is registered in Belgium under BCE/KBO 1045.010.593. Full details are in our legal notice.
  • No tax certificates. Aether EdTech is not currently approved to issue tax certificates, so donations are not tax-deductible. We will update this page if that changes.
  • Use of funds. Donations pay for hosting, software tools, development and educational content for our projects. We will publish an annual summary of how funds are used.
