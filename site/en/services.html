Services

Software and cybersecurity, with a purpose.

We develop software and protect the digital assets of schools, associations and companies. What remains after costs funds free educational projects.

How it works

Two sides of one organisation.

Free, always

Our public educational projects, starting with BacMath.eu. Open to every learner, funded by donations, partners and the services on this page.

Paid, for a reason

Software and cybersecurity work for organisations that need it. Fees cover our costs, and any surplus is reinvested in education, as the law for an ASBL requires.

  1. Websites and web applications

    Clear, fast, accessible sites and tools for schools, associations and small organisations.

  2. Educational software

    Learning platforms, quizzes, practice tools and content systems, built with a teacher's input from the first sketch.

  3. Cybersecurity

    We help organise and protect your digital assets: what you own, who can reach it, and how it is defended. Details below.

  4. Digital advice

    Practical guidance on choosing tools, going online and making digital services easier and safer to use.

Cybersecurity services

Know it. Secure it. Be ready.

Our cybersecurity work is built for organisations that do not have a security team. It is practical, plain-spoken and sized to your needs.

01

Asset inventory and classification

We list your devices, accounts, data, software and services, and rank them by how much they matter.

02

Vulnerability and configuration review

We check websites, applications, cloud settings and networks for weaknesses, and report them in plain language.

03

Secure development and code review

We build with security in from the start and review existing code before it reaches your users.

04

Account and access protection

Multi-factor authentication, sensible permissions, password practice and backups that actually restore.

05

Awareness training

Short, practical sessions for staff and teachers on phishing, scams and safe everyday habits.

06

Incident readiness

A simple, written plan for who does what when something goes wrong, so you lose less time and less data.

How we work. Every engagement starts with a conversation and a written scope. We test only systems we have written permission to test. Your information stays confidential. Fees support our educational projects.
