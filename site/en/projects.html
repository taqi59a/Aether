What we are building, and what we are joining.

Free educational resources are our public good. They are open to everyone and they come first.

Our first project · live now

BacMath.eu

BacMath.eu is an educational maths website that makes learning fun. It is the first Aether EdTech project and a clear picture of what we are for: take a subject many students dread, and make it something they can enjoy.

It is free to use. Hosting, development and new content cost time and money, which is why your support matters.
