About Aether EdTech

A Belgian non-profit for education and educational technology

Aether EdTech ASBL was founded in Brussels by Taqi Abbas and Minha Shahid, both educationists and teachers. We build free learning projects, provide software and cybersecurity services, and contribute to other education initiatives.

Mission & vision

Purpose and direction

Our mission

To support education by building free learning projects, contributing to educational initiatives led by others, and providing software and cybersecurity services that fund this work.

Our vision

Learning resources that are freely available, and educational technology that schools, organisations and learners can trust with their data.

Our work

What our work covers

Aether EdTech supports education and educational projects through three activities.

We create learning resources that are free for learners, and we build and maintain the software behind them. We provide software development and cybersecurity services to schools, associations and companies. We also contribute technical and teaching expertise to initiatives run by others.

Our principles

  • Grounded in teaching. Our projects and software are shaped by classroom experience.
  • Free access. Our core learning resources are free for learners.
  • Security and privacy by default. We collect only the data we need and protect it carefully.
  • Reinvestment. Income from our services is reinvested in educational work.
