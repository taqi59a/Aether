Cookies

Cookie statement.

This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What we use

We set no cookies ourselves, and we do not use local storage, fingerprinting, analytics, advertising or social media widgets.

Security cookie from our delivery network

Our site is delivered through Cloudflare. To protect it against abusive traffic, Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary technical cookie (for example __cf_bm). It is not used for tracking or advertising and does not require consent.

Fonts and resources

Fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself. We do not load them from other companies, so your browser does not contact third-party advertisers or analytics services.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that needs your consent, we will ask before it loads, as Belgian and EU law require, and we will update this page.
