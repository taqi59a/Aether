Education · Software · Cybersecurity · Brussels

Learning projects, software and cybersecurity, from a Brussels non-profit.

Aether EdTech ASBL builds free learning projects and provides software development and cybersecurity services to schools, associations and companies. Income from our services is reinvested in our educational work.

What we do

Three areas of work

Our learning projects are free. Our services are paid, and the income supports the projects.

  1. Learning projects

    Free educational websites and tools, designed by teachers. BacMath.eu, a mathematics learning website, is the first. Further projects are in development.

  2. Software and cybersecurity services

    Software development, security assessments and staff training for schools, associations and companies. Service income funds our educational projects.

  3. Partnerships

    We contribute software, technical expertise and teaching time to education projects run by schools, associations and community organisations.

Cybersecurity

Protect the systems and data your organisation relies on

Schools, associations and small companies hold personal data, financial records and online accounts that need protecting. We help you identify what you have, reduce the main risks and prepare your team to respond to incidents.

01

Asset mapping

An inventory of your devices, accounts, data and software, and of who has access to each.

02

Security assessments

A review of your websites, systems and configuration, with a prioritised list of fixes.

03

Secure development

Software designed and built with security requirements from the start.

04

Awareness and incident readiness

Training for staff and a written plan for responding to security incidents.
