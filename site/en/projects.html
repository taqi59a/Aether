Educational projects and partnerships

Our learning projects are free for learners. We also contribute to initiatives led by schools, associations and other organisations.

First project · live

BacMath.eu

BacMath.eu is a mathematics learning website with clear explanations and practice that students work through at their own pace. It reflects our approach to every project: grounded in teaching experience, free for learners, and carefully built and maintained.

BacMath.eu is free to use. Its hosting, development and new content are funded by service income, donations and partners.
