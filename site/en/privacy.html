Privacy & GDPR

Privacy policy and GDPR information.

Short version: no tracking, no advertising, your message is used only to reply to you, and our systems are in the European Union.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies or pixels.
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Our website, contact form and email sending run on Microsoft Azure in the European Union (North Europe, Ireland).
  • The site is delivered through Cloudflare’s network, which is explained below.
  • You can ask us at any time to access or delete your data.

Who is responsible

The controller of personal data is Aether EdTech ASBL, enterprise number BCE/KBO 1045.010.593, Schaerbeek, Brussels, Belgium. Contact: info@myaether.org. We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer; privacy questions go to the same address.

What we process, why, and for how long

Visiting this website

We do not collect personal data from visitors ourselves. Technically, our delivery network (Cloudflare) and hosting (Microsoft Azure) process your IP address and the time of your request in order to deliver the pages and protect the site against attacks. We do not use this to identify or profile you.

Language selection

The language is chosen by a small script that reads your browser’s language setting on your own device. This information is not sent to us and nothing is stored.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address, chosen topic and message are sent over an encrypted connection to a small program on Microsoft Azure in the EU. It forwards your message to our mailbox and sends you an automatic confirmation email in French and English. Nothing is stored in a database and we do not log the content of your message. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or steps you ask us to take before an agreement (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR). Messages stay in our mailbox only as long as needed to handle your request, normally no longer than 24 months after the last exchange.

When you email us directly

We process your name, address and message to reply, on the same legal basis and for the same period.

Donations by bank transfer

Your bank gives us your name, account number and the amount. We keep this to meet our accounting obligations under Belgian law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), normally for seven years.

Who processes data for us

  • Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd (Microsoft Azure): website storage, the contact-form program and email sending, in the EU (North Europe, Ireland; email data location Europe).
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: DNS, delivery network and security for myaether.org. Cloudflare operates a global network and may process visitors’ IP addresses outside the EEA. Such transfers rely on the EU standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Our mailbox provider for info@myaether.org.

They act only on our instructions under data processing terms. We do not sell data and we do not use advertising or analytics services.

Where your data is kept

The files of this website, the contact-form program and email sending are in the European Union. The only processing we know of outside the EEA is the technical delivery of pages through Cloudflare’s global network, described above. If this changes, we will update this page first.

Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Write to info@myaether.org. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

Right to complain

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, contact@apd-gba.be, autoriteprotectiondonnees.be. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website. BacMath.eu is a separate website with its own privacy information.

Links to other websites

Links to BacMath.eu and other sites lead to services we do not control for this website. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave.

Security

We limit access to the mailbox to the founders, use encrypted connections throughout and choose reputable providers. No method is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the authority as the GDPR requires.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the date at the top. Material changes will be announced on the home page.
